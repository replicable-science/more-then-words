## 2. Additional checks (not reported as manuscript numbers)

- **Zero crossing vs. significance threshold.** The estimated slope of facial energy turns positive at the 38th percentile of negative words; it becomes significant (Johnson-Neyman) at the 65th.
- **Boundary on the probability scale.** The 95% simulation band of the funding gain excludes zero from the 6600th percentile on (log-odds scale: 65th).
- **Bootstrap draws without a region.** In 7% of the 1,000 bootstrap draws, passion pays significantly nowhere; the 38th-to-92nd interval for the threshold refers to the other draws. Median share of pitches in the region: 34% (95% interval 0% to 65%).
- **Small subsets and separation.** Logit fits that did not converge or have a coefficient beyond 10 in absolute value (a dummy perfectly predicts funding), out of 400 subsets each: about 100 pitches 228; about 150 pitches 117; about 230 pitches 20; about 300 pitches 1. Share significant among the clean fits only: 18%, 27%, 61%, 78% (all fits: 19%, 28%, 61%, 78%). Older statsmodels versions (0.14) stop with an error on some of these fits and drop them, which moves two of the all-fits shares by one point. Bootstrap draws that did not converge: 6 of 1,000; both random-half fits converged: yes.
- **Resolution in time.** Facial energy measured in each third of the pitch, times negative words: first third b = 0.30 (0.09), p = .001; second third b = 0.24 (0.10), p = .014; last third b = 0.30 (0.10), p = .003. Facial energy correlates .79, .77, .78 across thirds.
- **Is the jingle just unreliability?** Alpha across thirds: facial energy .91, vocal energy .95, verbal arousal .44, joyful words .46. Jingle correlations corrected for attenuation: facial energy with vocal energy .13, facial energy with joyful words -.01, vocal energy with joyful words .08.
- **How large could the pruned effects be?** 95% confidence intervals (log-odds per SD): joy [-0.18, 0.21]; smile [-0.18, 0.29]; positive face [-0.07, 0.36]; positive-face index [-0.16, 0.29]; index x negative words [-0.19, 0.18].
- **Events per parameter.** 156 funded pitches, 26 parameters in Table 2, Model 3 (6.0 events per parameter). Sparse controls (no industry dummies, no season effects, 12 parameters): interaction b = 0.28 (0.10), p = .004; slope at the 90th percentile 0.67, at the 10th 0.06; Johnson-Neyman share 62%.
- **BIC.** Controls 699.5, + six cues 723.2, + joint term 721.2 (lower is better; the manuscript reports AIC only).
- **Random half A.** Passion is significantly negative in 0% of pitches.
